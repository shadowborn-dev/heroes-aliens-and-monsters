Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before you get in touch.

What if I'm looking for a rare item?+
No matter what you're looking for, Heroes Aliens and Monsters can locate it for you. Get in touch with details of the item and we'll start the search.
Can I order online?+
Not yet. Orders are taken by phone, email or the contact form, and payment is fast and simple via PayPal once we've confirmed what you need.
Do you hold in-shop events?+
Yes. We frequently host events with guest celebrities in store, ranging from the cast of Doctor Who to well-known faces from TV series of previous decades. Details are shared on our News & Events page.
How can I learn about new promotions?+
New items and promotions are showcased regularly here, so it's worth checking back often for the latest finds.
What payment methods do you accept?+
Easy and secure payments via PayPal. For in-store purchases, ask about other options when you visit.
Where are you based?+
53 Tylacelyn Road, Tonypandy, CF40 1JU, Wales, within easy reach of Cardiff and Swansea. Full details on our Contact page.
