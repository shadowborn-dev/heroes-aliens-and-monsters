Mission Briefing

Over a decade of finding the unfindable.

Heroes Aliens and Monsters has spent over a decade tracking down the memorabilia other shops give up on. From model kits and figurines to TV-related books, DVDs and the odd Dalek, if it's sci-fi, it's probably passed through our hands.

Whatever you're after, a long-discontinued trading card set, a specific Doctor Who annual, the perfect gift for a Trekkie who already has everything, chances are we can find it. We know the value of what we sell, and we know how to track down the pieces everyone else has given up on.

We also take the collection on the road, attending local comic cons and memorabilia fairs across Wales, and we regularly welcome guest celebrities for in-store signing events. See our Celebrity Catch Ups page for the full roll call.
