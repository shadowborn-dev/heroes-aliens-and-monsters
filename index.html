Mission Briefing

For everything out of this world.

Based in Tonypandy, within easy reach of Cardiff and Swansea, Heroes Aliens and Monsters has spent over a decade tracking down the memorabilia other shops give up on. From model kits and figurines to TV-related books, DVDs and the odd Dalek, if it's sci-fi, it's probably passed through our hands.

Whatever you're after, a long-discontinued trading card set, a specific Doctor Who annual, the perfect gift for a Trekkie who already has everything, chances are we can find it.
